# Preliminary brand naming research

## Decision

Use **Larevia** as the working Brokerage Brand and **Maia** as its named
assistant. Do not introduce a separate Platform Brand during the brokerage MVP.

Larevia is a coined name built from *Lar/Lares*, associated with the home and its
guardian in Latin tradition, and *via*, a path or way. The intended commercial
idea is a trusted path toward a home. The construction is a naming rationale,
not a claim about a historical Latin word.

The customer-facing expression should stay plain:

> Larevia, con Maia como tu asistente inmobiliaria.

## Preliminary collision screen

An exact-name web search performed on 2026-08-27 did not surface a material
Mexican real-estate or software operator named Larevia. `larevia.mx` and
`larevia.com.mx` also returned no RDAP registration record at the time of the
check, while `larevia.com` was registered. These are weak screening signals, not
proof that a domain or trademark is available.

Before paying for identity work or launching publicly, search exact and similar
marks in the Mexican Institute of Industrial Property's
[MARCia](https://marcia.impi.gob.mx/marcas/search) database and obtain a
professional clearance opinion. The likely Nice classes to examine include
Class 36 for real-estate services and, when a B2B software offer is real, Class
42 for SaaS; the exact filing scope requires trademark counsel.

## Why the Platform Brand is deferred

The first proof is the brokerage operation, not a self-service SaaS product.
Naming a second platform now would add a third identity beside Larevia and Maia,
create avoidable explanation for customers, and assume a B2B position before
customer discovery validates it. The system can retain clean organizational and
service boundaries without exposing those boundaries as brands.
