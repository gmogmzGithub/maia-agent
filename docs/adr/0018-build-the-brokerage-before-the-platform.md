# Build the brokerage before the platform

Maia will first support the real-estate operation run by Santiago and the
advisors who later join his team. We will preserve clear organizational and
domain boundaries so the product can later become a multi-agency platform, but
we will not build or sell the SaaS product in parallel with proving the
technology-enabled brokerage. This chooses operational learning and measurable
sales outcomes over prematurely serving two markets with different onboarding,
isolation, support, and commercial requirements.
