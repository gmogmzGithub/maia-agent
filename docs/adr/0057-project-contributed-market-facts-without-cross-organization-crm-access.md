# Project contributed market facts without cross-Organization CRM access

Brokerage Organizations will manually capture new Purchase Profiles and Market
Sale Records from the moment this capability launches; Product will not import
history or ingest external public, cadastral, registry, demographic or listing
feeds. Each operational record remains owned and scoped by its Organization.
A separate durable Market Contribution projects the analytical buyer, Property
and sale fields into a Platform-wide Shared Market Dataset, including Paid Price,
Published Price and Appraisal Value, while excluding Contact identifiers,
channel identities, documents and conversations. This permits the operating
company to build cross-Organization comparables and market intelligence without
querying one Organization's CRM from another Organization or weakening ADR-0050.
Brokerage users see their own detailed contributions and shared derived
statistics, not another Organization's individual operational record; a Market
Intelligence Analyst may analyze the shared dataset without receiving CRM access.
Contributions from two Organizations remain separately attributable but may be
resolved as one shared sale after human review, so a co-brokered deal is counted
once. Cancelled Transaction Journeys may contribute their known analytical facts
and outcome, but only Administrator-confirmed completed records with a Paid Price
may become Comparable Sales. Exact contributed values are retained centrally;
reports derive ranges and disclose sample size, and aggregate statistics require
at least five applicable completed records.
