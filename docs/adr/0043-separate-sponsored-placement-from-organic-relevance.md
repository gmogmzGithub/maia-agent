# Separate Sponsored Placement from organic relevance

The MVP sells manually administered 30-day Sponsorship Campaigns to eligible
property owners, Developers, or External Collaborators for search, homepage, or
both. The buyer purchases defined additional visibility, not guaranteed leads,
appointments, transactions, or preferential Advisor service. Unpaid editorial
selection uses `Destacada`, never `Patrocinada`.

A Sponsored Placement must satisfy every hard customer criterion and all ordinary
authority, availability, publication, media, and Presentation Readiness rules.
Payment does not change Presentation Tier and never influences Maia's matching or
recommendations. Search displays at most one sponsored result per six visible
results; the homepage displays at most two in a dedicated section. Every instance
is explicitly and accessibly labeled `Patrocinada`.

Product pauses a campaign when its Listing becomes ineligible and preserves
remaining active days. One confirmed physical Property may occupy only one
sponsored result position, while campaign ownership, commercial attribution, and
lead routing remain attached to the paying source Listing. Each Brokerage
Organization controls its own sponsored surfaces; a cross-organization advertising
network is out of scope.

Reporting separates Opportunity Origin, Sponsored Exposure, Sponsored Engagement,
and known downstream outcomes. The Administrator sees operational and commercial
detail; buyers receive only campaign-scoped or safely aggregated evidence. Neither
surface exposes Contact identity, message content, or unsupported causal claims.

Campaign state is Draft, Quoted, Reserved, Scheduled, Active, Paused, Completed,
or Cancelled. Quotes expire after seven days without reserving capacity. Product
forecasts sellable capacity, rotates qualified campaigns toward equitable delivery,
and limits a Listing to three paid Visible Impressions per anonymous session per
day. Buyers cannot provide separate advertising creative.

Pricing is an Administrator-managed versioned catalog for search, homepage, and
both. Quotes preserve their catalog version and may include a reasoned manual
discount. The MVP records external collection state but does not auction positions,
charge per click, move money, issue invoices, or automate tax accounting.

Campaigns do not auto-renew. Daily and per-exposure eligibility checks may pause
delivery while preserving paid days for an Administrator-recorded resolution.
Initial prices follow measured pilot traffic rather than an unsupported forecast.
