# Assign Listing presentation by price, not customer

The Platform assigns every house or apartment Listing a Larevia, Premium, or
Super Premium Presentation Tier from its manually entered asking price or monthly
rent and organization-configured thresholds. It never selects an experience from
the Contact's identity, behavior, inferred wealth, or socioeconomic status. An
Organization Administrator may override the automatic tier explicitly.

Any active Offer denominated in USD contributes Premium regardless of its amount.
A Listing with several active Offers uses the highest contributed tier unless an
Administrator overrides it. Product performs and displays no currency conversion.
An override requires no typed reason, but the ordinary audit log records actor and
time automatically.

The Larevia tier remains an excellent complete experience. Premium and Super
Premium use their own single product-owned templates, changing editorial art
direction, visual sequencing, typography, transitions, composition, and accessible
palette without changing factual accuracy, performance, accessibility, sharing,
Maia access, or appointment capability. Administrators choose the tier but cannot
customize colors or create per-Listing themes.

Product separately runs deterministic Presentation Readiness checks for required
cover, approved media, and authorized facts; it does not ask Maia or an AI model
to judge photographic taste. The Administrator may override readiness. Thresholds
are reviewed every six months and initially apply only to houses and apartments.

Monthly rent is always entered manually and is the only price used to assign a
rental Listing's Commercial Price Band and Presentation Tier.

Readiness initially requires 6 approved photographs for Larevia, 12 photographs
across 4 space groups plus a high-resolution cover for Premium, and 20 photographs
across 6 groups plus a high-resolution cover for Super Premium; all tiers require
complete authorized facts. A price change recalculates the tier unless an explicit
Administrator override remains active. Gallery and Technical Sheet URLs stay
stable through tier changes.

An Administrator may manually hide an Offer's exact price from public surfaces;
the internal price remains required and the site shows `Precio disponible previa
consulta` with a `Solicitar precio` action. One Listing and stable URL may contain
both sale and rental Offers sharing media and facts while keeping their prices,
currencies, visibility, terms, and availability separate. Maia may disclose a
hidden public price when the Contact requests it privately; the price remains
eligible for authorized matching and filtering.
