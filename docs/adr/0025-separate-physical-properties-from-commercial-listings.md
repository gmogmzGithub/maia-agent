# Separate physical Properties from commercial Listings

A Property represents a physical real-estate asset or unit, while each Listing
represents one source-specific publication with its own authority, attribution,
media, and one or more sale or rental Offers. Each Offer retains its own price,
currency, public price visibility, terms, and availability. Product will not
flatten Organization and collaborator publications into one apparent Listing or
automatically merge Listings whose shared physical identity is unproven; this
preserves provenance when EasyBroker and the brokerage describe the same asset
differently.

When an Organization Listing and a Collaborator Listing are confirmed to represent
the same Property, the Organization Listing is the preferred commercial path and
its Property Expert retains priority. The source-specific Listing remains available
for provenance rather than being silently overwritten.

One Listing may contain both a sale Offer and a rental Offer under the same public
URL and shared gallery. Offer availability remains independent: a sale disables
both Offers, while a rental disables the rental Offer and leaves continuation of
the sale Offer to the Administrator.
