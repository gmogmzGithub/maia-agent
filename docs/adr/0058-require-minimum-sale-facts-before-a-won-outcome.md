# Require minimum sale facts before a Won outcome

An Organization Administrator may mark a purchase Opportunity Won only when its
Market Sale Record names the Property, property type, municipality, completion
date, Paid Price and currency. Published Price, Appraisal Value and the remaining
Property comparison facts stay optional and explicitly distinguish not provided
from not yet captured. This deliberately prevents the operation from closing an
otherwise valid CRM outcome until it records the minimum facts needed to build
the company's sale-history asset; unfinished and cancelled journeys remain useful
analytical records but never appear as completed or Comparable Sales.
