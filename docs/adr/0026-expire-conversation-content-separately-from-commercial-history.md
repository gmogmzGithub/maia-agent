# Expire conversation content separately from commercial history

After 90 consecutive days without interaction, Maia closes the Hermes
Conversational Session and expires full message bodies by default; later contact
starts a new session rather than replaying the old chat. Structured commercial
facts survive only under purpose-specific retention: Property Needs become Stale
and require reconfirmation, while Contacts, Opportunity outcomes, consent,
Suppression Records, attribution, and minimal audit evidence may be retained long
enough to operate lawfully and avoid forbidden outreach. Exact exceptions and
periods remain subject to Mexican legal review.
