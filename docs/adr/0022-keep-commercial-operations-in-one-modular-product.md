# Keep commercial operations in one modular product

CRM, Contacts, Opportunities, assignment, follow-up, inventory, appointments,
integration policy, and operational analytics will begin as modules of one Maia
Product sharing PostgreSQL transactions. The public site and Hermes remain
separate runtime processes, but hypothetical future licensing does not justify
premature CRM, campaign, EasyBroker, or analytics microservices; extraction will
require a concrete scaling, security, availability, or team-ownership need.
