# Build a managed real-estate platform, not a general CRM

The future external offering will productize the real-estate operating model as
a managed multi-organization service whose promise is follow-up coverage, not as a
generic configurable CRM or one dedicated server per customer. Each Brokerage
Organization owns and is isolated to its identifiable operating data and provider
credentials; onboarding and integrations remain assisted until repeated customer
work proves what can safely become self-service.
