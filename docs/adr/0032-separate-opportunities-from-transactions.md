# Separate Opportunities from Transactions

An Opportunity represents the commercial effort to satisfy one Property Need;
a Transaction represents a deal that actually completes or is completing. Only
an Organization Administrator may mark an Opportunity Won from accepted legal or
commercial evidence, after which a Transaction records the parties, Property or
Listing, dates, price, attribution, and manually known commission facts. Maia
never infers a sale from conversational progress, and the MVP does not calculate
or distribute commissions.
