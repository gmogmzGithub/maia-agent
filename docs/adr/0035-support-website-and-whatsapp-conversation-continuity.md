# Support Website and WhatsApp conversation continuity

Customers may converse with Maia Agent anonymously on the public site or continue
through WhatsApp without creating an account. Product may associate both
channel-specific conversations with one Opportunity, but it links them to one
Contact only after identity verification; transitions use a short-lived signed
opaque handoff rather than personal data or transcript content in a URL. Human
Handling, consent, audit, and 90-day conversation expiration apply across both
channels.
