# Make Product the commercial system of record

Product owns authoritative Contacts, Property Needs, Opportunities, consent,
assignments, next actions, communication restrictions, and Commercial Outcomes.
EasyBroker may exchange prospect or attribution data when an integration contract
requires it, but it does not co-own editable commercial state; this avoids two
systems independently changing the same customer relationship and makes Product's
operating promise measurable.
