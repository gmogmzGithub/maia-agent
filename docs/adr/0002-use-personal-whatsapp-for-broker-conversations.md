# Use personal WhatsApp for broker conversations

Brokers conduct human customer conversations through their personal WhatsApp
accounts. The initial product does not require storing or synchronizing those
conversations; brokers record customer progress in the CRM. This simplifies
the initial human workflow at the cost of automatic visibility into broker
messages, so takeover and resumption cannot depend on observing those messages.
The CRM controls “Atención del asesor” and “Reactivar Maia” explicitly pause and
resume automatic replies and customer follow-up. Internal reminders remain active.
