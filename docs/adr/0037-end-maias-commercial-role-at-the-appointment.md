# End Maia's commercial role at the appointment

Maia qualifies the Contact, answers from authorized Property facts, and works
toward the most important conversion: a confirmed appointment. That confirmation
creates an Appointment Handoff. From then onward, the Real Estate Advisor owns
the customer relationship and all subsequent service, negotiation, documents,
offers, reservations, contracts, and closing. Maia may still perform bounded
Appointment Logistics: confirming, reminding, rescheduling, or cancelling against
authoritative Advisor availability.

Product routes clear appointment-logistics messages to Maia, commercial or visit
questions to the Advisor, and ambiguous post-handoff messages to the Advisor.
Rescheduling first secures the new slot and only then releases the old one; a
failure preserves the original appointment. Cancellation does not determine the
Opportunity outcome, and Maia asks only once whether the Contact wants another
time.

Maia may converse with a Contact in any requested language before the handoff,
but Advisors initially provide human service in Spanish. Maia discloses that
limitation before booking and does not become a translation relay between the
Contact and Advisor. This keeps Maia focused on lead conversion and avoids
presenting conversational output as legal, negotiating, or transactional
authority.

After a visit, the Advisor records what occurred and the next commercial action;
Product may remind the Advisor internally, but Maia does not pursue the Contact.
An Administrator-authorized new-inventory match may later start a new Maia
conversation whose objective is a new appointment rather than continuation of
the prior negotiation. A Missed Appointment produces a Maia rescheduling
invitation only when the Advisor explicitly authorizes it.

The MVP automates only a confirmed 90-minute visit connecting one Contact, one
Advisor, and one Property. It does not model soft appointments, calls, video or
office meetings, multi-property tours, or traffic calculation. Advisors express
travel needs as busy time in their authoritative calendars. Accompanying visitor
details remain optional unless a Property's access policy requires them.
