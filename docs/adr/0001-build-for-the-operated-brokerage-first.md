# Build for the operated brokerage first

The initial business is an operated brokerage earning transaction commissions,
starting with a small pilot and growing its broker team. Product scope will be
driven by that operation rather than by the customization needs of hypothetical
software customers. Preserve the ability to serve independent brokerages later;
offering the full platform or a connected agent as SaaS is a future option to
evaluate after validating the product in the initial brokerage, not an initial
delivery requirement.
