# Let Maia coordinate human-confirmed Transaction Journeys

ADR-0037 ended Maia's commercial role at the appointment to keep negotiation,
documents and closing under human authority. Maia may now re-enter after an
authorized Organization Member explicitly starts a Transaction Journey in the
CRM: Product instantiates a versioned milestone plan, humans confirm every
material state and its evidence, and Maia communicates those confirmed states,
pending work, reminders, delays and bounded explanations to the Contact. Maia
does not negotiate, interpret documents legally, approve financing, infer that a
milestone occurred or declare a deal complete. Starting the Journey does not mark
the Opportunity Won; Won remains the Administrator-confirmed terminal outcome of
a legally completed sale, signed rental agreement or accepted binding presale.
