# Treat EasyBroker as secondary authorized inventory

Product will search the Brokerage Organization's own controlled inventory before
eligible EasyBroker collaborator inventory. EasyBroker remains a replaceable,
organization-authorized external source whose records preserve provenance,
freshness, attribution, collaboration authority, and commercial conditions; it
is neither Product's system of record nor a guarantee of access to every property
in Mexico. Production scope remains conditional on EasyBroker's written
commercial and data-use permissions.
