# Make follow-up state-driven

Maia will determine follow-up from the Opportunity's current state, next action,
consent, recent interaction, appointment state, and newly available value rather
than sending every Contact the same fixed 28-day sequence. Santiago's proposed
cadence may be evaluated as a versioned experiment, but it is not universal
product truth; replies, appointments, rejection, opt-out, dormancy, and relevant
new inventory must change or stop the sequence deterministically.

The first conservative hypothesis for an unanswered inquiry is an immediate
response followed, when permitted, by WhatsApp contacts on days 1, 3, 7, 14, and
28. Any reply ends that generic sequence, and a Qualified Opportunity uses its
contextual Next Actions instead. Silence after day 28 produces Dormant with a
recorded reason rather than Lost. Calls are human activities recorded in the CRM;
automated email follow-up is deferred from the MVP.
