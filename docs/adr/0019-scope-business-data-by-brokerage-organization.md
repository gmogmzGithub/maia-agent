# Scope business data by Brokerage Organization

Even while Maia operates for one brokerage, business records and external
credentials will belong explicitly to a Brokerage Organization rather than to an
implicit global account. This keeps Contacts, Advisors, Opportunities,
consents, Properties, and provider access from becoming inseparable from
Santiago's initial operation while avoiding premature SaaS provisioning or one
deployment per future customer.
