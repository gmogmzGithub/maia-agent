# Limit initial operations to the Guadalajara Service Area

The Brokerage Organization will initially operate only in the Guadalajara
Metropolitan Area, limited to Guadalajara, Zapopan, and Tlaquepaque. Out-of-area
search, referral, and transaction workflows are deferred; Maia records the
limitation and does not promise advice, Listings, visits, or transaction support
outside the Service Area merely because an external source contains inventory.
