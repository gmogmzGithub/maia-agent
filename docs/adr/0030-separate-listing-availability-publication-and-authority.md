# Separate Listing availability, publication, and authority

Listing Availability, Publication State, and Authority are independent business
facts: a published Listing may be reserved, an available Listing may be private,
and an EasyBroker-visible Listing may lack current external-use authority. Maia
may present, publish, recommend, or schedule against a Listing only when each
dimension independently permits that action rather than relying on one overloaded
Active flag.
