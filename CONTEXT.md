# Maia Domain Language

Business vocabulary established during the fresh product review.

## Language

**Brokerage (Inmobiliaria)**:
A business that brokers the sale or rental of real estate.

**Broker (Asesor inmobiliario)**:
A person who assists customers with real estate transactions on behalf of a brokerage.

**Brokerage administrator (Administrador de la inmobiliaria)**:
A person authorized to manage the brokerage's property inventory beyond their
own assigned properties. A broker may also hold this role.

**Property submitter (Persona que registra un inmueble)**:
An account holder who submits a property to the brokerage through the website.
Submitting does not itself establish broker status or ownership of the property.

**Property submission (Solicitud para comercializar un inmueble)**:
A request for the brokerage to help sell or rent a property, subject to review
before becoming a published listing.

**Listing withdrawal (Retiro de publicación)**:
Removing a listing from customer discovery and new visit bookings while
preserving its business history. Withdrawal does not itself cancel existing
appointments.

**Lead (Prospecto)**:
A person who has expressed interest in a brokerage's real estate services or inventory.

**Lead follow-up (Seguimiento de prospectos)**:
Continued attention to a lead after an initial inquiry, intended to help them progress toward a suitable real estate transaction.

**Visit appointment (Cita de visita)**:
An agreed time for a prospective customer to visit a property with a responsible broker.

**Transaction intent (Intención de operación)**:
A prospective customer's intention to pursue the purchase or rental of a specific property, confirmed by a broker. It does not establish that an agreement has been signed, a payment has been made, or the transaction has been completed.

**Shortlist (Selección de inmuebles)**:
A set of properties a prospective customer is considering for one search need. Inclusion expresses interest, not a request for contact or a commitment to transact.

**Property pursuit (Búsqueda comercial)**:
One distinct purchase or rental need pursued by a customer, potentially involving several candidate properties. A customer may have several independent pursuits.

**Responsible broker (Asesor responsable del inmueble)**:
The broker assigned to a property who is responsible for its inquiries and visits.

**Brokerage inventory (Inventario de la inmobiliaria)**:
The listings the brokerage manages and offers to customers, including listings
registered directly and listings supplied through its EasyBroker account.
The source of a listing does not create a separate brokerage responsibility.

**Property visit window (Horario de visitas del inmueble)**:
The recurring weekly periods during which a property permits visits, distinct from the responsible broker's calendar availability.

**Human takeover (Atención del asesor)**:
A conversation mode in which a broker handles the customer and automated replies
and customer follow-up are paused until Maia is explicitly reactivated.

**Desired characteristic (Característica deseada)**:
A property attribute or quantity the customer prefers, such as three bedrooms.
It describes a target, not an automatic minimum or a mandatory condition.

**Hard requirement (Requisito indispensable)**:
A customer condition that must be satisfied for a property to be presented as
a qualifying match, distinct from a preference that may admit explained alternatives.
